01

I remember the beginning

We had been classmates since 9th standard. I remember seeing you for the first time and somehow feeling something I couldn't name properly yet. I waited. Then 12th came, and suddenly you changed schools. I genuinely thought that was it—that somehow the person I had quietly carried in my heart for years was just going to become a memory.

02

And then you came back

Somehow, you came back. Looking back at it now, it still feels like one of those strange little plot twists life gives you when you least expect them. I don't want to turn that into some grand claim about destiny. I just know that I was grateful you were there again.

03

The day we finally talked

Scribble day. Everyone was writing on each other's lab coats, and I wanted an excuse to talk to you. I wrote “solace” and “ephemeral” on yours. I was supposed to write “sempiternal,” but I deliberately wrote “ephemeral” so that I could text you later and ask you to correct it. It was such a stupid little plan—and somehow it worked.

04

Before that, there were already little moments

I still remember the physics practical when I had absolutely no idea what I was doing and you helped me through it. You tolerated a version of me that could be genuinely rude and clueless. I don't think I appreciated that enough then.

05

The little things became everything

I remember that Monday when there were no classes and we went for self-study. We studied until around 2:30, and then came the donuts. I fed you, you fed me, and somehow even sharing the same spoon became a memory I still smile about.

06

The bus ride

I remember you being tired and stressed and wanting to sleep. And then you held my right hand tightly and rested your head on my shoulder. I remember how that felt—not because it was some dramatic moment, but because it felt like you trusted me enough to be that comfortable with me.

07

Chetak Bridge

I remember the first time we held hands there. We walked the entire bridge, talking about everything and nothing. You yapped, I listened, and somehow I never wanted those conversations to end. And then there were all those tuition bus rides—sitting beside each other and talking endlessly on the way home.

08

Our weird little telepathy

There were so many times when we would say the same thing at the same time, eat gulab jamun at the same moment, somehow have the same phone-battery percentage, or just think of the same thing without planning it.

09

What I don't understand

I know things changed. I know you felt that our thinking didn't match, and I know there were moments when you wanted peace, wanted to focus on studies, or felt like you were losing interest. What I don't want to do is pretend I know exactly what was happening inside you when I don't.

10

I owe you a real apology

I didn't treat you the way you deserved all the time. I was rude sometimes. I didn't always communicate properly. I didn't always understand what you needed from me, and there were moments when I should have been more patient, more thoughtful, and simply better at being there for you.

11

Even the small things mattered

I didn't even know about GF Day, so I didn't post you. At the time it was simply something I didn't know existed. But I understand now that what looks like a small thing to one person can carry a completely different meaning for the other person.

12

What I miss

I miss your reassuring VNs. I miss the random lovey-dovey paragraphs. And strangely, I even miss the way you would get angry and confront me—because at least you were telling me what was wrong instead of silently carrying it away.

13

What I would do differently

If I ever get the chance to do this again, I don't want to simply promise that I'll be better. I want to understand what being better actually means. I want to listen without immediately defending myself. I want to communicate instead of assuming. I want to notice when something is bothering you instead of waiting until it becomes too big. I want to respect your feelings even when I don't immediately understand them.

14

This isn't about pretending nothing happened

I don't want a restart that erases everything that happened before. I don't want to say “forget it” and pretend we were perfect. We weren't. I wasn't. And I know there are things I have to take responsibility for.

“I don't want to argue with what you felt. I want to understand it.”
I owe you this

What I learned.

I didn't even know about GF Day, so I didn't post you. At the time, it was simply something I didn't know existed. But I understand now that what looks small to one person can carry a completely different meaning for the other person.

If I ever get another chance, I don't want to simply promise that I'll be better. I want to listen without immediately defending myself. Communicate instead of assuming. Notice when something is bothering you. Respect your feelings even when I don't immediately understand them.

I can't honestly promise that I'll never make another mistake. But I can promise that I won't take you or your feelings for granted.
